### #rules

```
**The rules**

1. **Be decent.** No harassment, hate, slurs or personal attacks. Disagree about ideas, not people.
2. **No game files.** No client downloads, repacks, torrents or links to them. Bring your own game.
3. **Nobody here will ever ask for your password or your Hero Key.** If someone does, it isn't us: report them to a Moderator.
4. **No selling, no ads, no spam.** No paid services, no begging, no invite spam. Hosts: tell people about your realm in #list-your-realm only.
5. **Keep it in the right channel.** Bugs in #bug-reports, help in the help channels, chat in #hangout.
6. **No cheats or exploits.** Found one? Report it privately to a Moderator, don't post how.
7. **Bots are labelled.** Our bots and the game's companion bots always say they are bots.

One ban covers your whole Leyline account: the game, the website, the forum and this Discord.

## Your Den

### #den-showcase

```
Show your **Den** here: a screenshot or two and a line about it.

Every week the team picks a **Den of the week**. It is posted here, shows on the website and in the launcher, and its builder gets the **Den Architect** role.

New to Dens? In the launcher, open **Your Den** and press **Play**. In your Den, **F7** is edit mode. Questions go in #den-help.
```

### #den-files (how to share a Den)

```
**How to share your Den**

1. In your Den, save it: **.den save <name>** (or **Save a copy** under Saved Dens in the launcher).
2. In the launcher, open **Your Den**, then **Share on the Workshop**, and follow the steps there.
3. Post the Workshop link here with a line about your Den.

**To open someone's Den:** copy their link, then in the launcher, **Your Den** > **Saved Dens** > **Add from a link**.

Links to the Workshop only, please. One post a minute.
```

### #den-help

```
Questions about building your Den: edit mode (**F7**), placing and moving props, paths, buildings, saving and loading, inviting a friend.

Tell us what you tried and what happened. A screenshot helps.

In the game: **.den save <name>** and **.den load <name>** keep layouts; **.den files** lists what you saved.

### #hosting-help (how to host)

```
**How to host your own realm**

1. In the launcher, open **Host**.
2. Pick a ruleset (or a template from #host-templates) and press **Start**. The first start takes a while.
3. When it says **Online**, copy your **invite link** and send it to your friends.
4. Want strangers to find it? Make it **public**: it shows in the realm list and in #live-realms.

Hosting on a server instead of your PC? The Host pack and its guide: https://leylinelauncher.com/wrath/host

**Asking for help?** Say what you tried, what you see, and paste the result of the **health check** from the Host page. Never post passwords, tokens or your settings files.
```

## Feedback

### #bug-reports (how to report a bug)

```
**How to report a bug**

One message per bug. Say:
1. **What you did** (the steps)
2. **What you expected**
3. **What happened instead**
4. **Where**: the realm and zone, the launcher page, or the website page
A screenshot helps.

**Every message here goes on the team's bug list**, together with reports from the game and the website, so the same bug reported twice is one item. Replying to a report adds yours to it.

Other ways:
- In the game: **.bug** and what happened, e.g. **.bug the quest giver in Northshire has no ! over his head** (it saves where you are)
- On the website: https://leylinelauncher.com/wrath/bugs

Never post passwords, your email or your Hero Key. Found an exploit? Tell a Moderator privately instead.
```
