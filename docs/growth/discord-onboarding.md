## 2. Roles and how they are earned

From docs/DISCORD.md (the nightly role sync, `WU_DISCORD_ROLES=1`). Nobody asks for a role; it follows what they did.

| Role | Earned by | Unlocks | Growth post that celebrates it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Realm Host** | a realm from the launcher's Host page, listed on the network for 7 days | host-lounge (later), the hosts' spotlight | Realm of the week |
| **Den Architect** | a Den of the week | kept for good | Den of the week |
| **Builder** | staff record a builder approval (HQ > Content > Discord > Record a fact) | | Den spotlight |
| **Mode Maker** | a game mode published (recorded in HQ) | jam-judges (later) | Feature reel of their mode, with their OK |
| **Node Keeper** | running a community node (recorded in HQ until nodes have accounts) | node-keepers (later) | State of Leyline's thank-you |
| **Guide** | by hand: people who help in #help | Manage Threads in #help | State of Leyline's thank-you |
| **Moderator**, **Core Team** | by hand | | |

## 3. Where the growth posts go

| Post | Discord channel | Who posts | Also |
|---|---|---|---|
| Patch notes | #announcements | the owner (or the website bot, from a forum announcement) | Reddit |
| Feature reel | #showcase (the 16:9 MP4 or the GIF) | the owner or a Moderator | Reddit, Shorts/Reels/TikTok |
| Guide episode | #guides-and-faq (pin the evergreen ones: 1, 3, 5, 11) | a Moderator or Guide | Reddit |
| Den of the week | #showcase | the owner or a Moderator | Reddit, with the builder's OK |
| Realm of the week | #host-a-realm | the owner or a Moderator | Reddit, with the host's OK |
| State of Leyline | #announcements | the owner | Reddit |
| Seeded season | #announcements, then a thread in #general | the owner | Reddit ([seeded-season.md](seeded-season.md)) |

The website bot already posts by itself when turned on (DISCORD-PLAN.md): Find a group in #looking-for-group, new mods
and the featured mode in #mods-and-workshop, community realms going live in #host-a-realm, Den of the week in
#showcase. The spotlight posts above add a human line on top of those, not a second copy.

## 4. Keeping it friendly

- Each channel's pin says what goes there in one line; off-topic is moved, not scolded.
- #help answers link the guide's page (reddit-guides.md has them), so answers stay the same everywhere.
- A thank-you by name in each State of Leyline: hosts, node keepers, Guides (with their OK).
- Bug reports: every message in #bug-reports lands on the bug list; when one is fixed, the patch notes say so, and a
  Guide can reply in the thread ("fixed in <date>'s update").
