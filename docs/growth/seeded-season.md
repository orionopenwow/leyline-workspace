**What it is**
- A fresh realm for the original-era client, starting from level 1. (S1)
- The first season covers levels 1 to 20: the starting zones, the capitals, the zones up to 20 and three early
  dungeons. The cap rises in phases as the season goes on. (S2)
- Server-side tuning only: you play with your own game folder and approved addons, nothing to patch.

**Who runs it**
- One host runs the world at a time, but never one place: two or more **Keepers** hold a live copy and can take
  over in minutes, planned or after a crash.
- The Leyline relay only connects players and keeps the score of who the host is. It holds no copy of the realm.
- Keepers are players who volunteer a PC that stays on. TODO(owner): how to become one. Draft: "Ask in
  #host-a-realm; after two weeks and one practice handover, you're in." (S5)

**What "Preview" means**
- A handover can drop you for a minute or two; the realm saves every minute, so a crash loses at most that.
- If something goes wrong we'll say so in #announcements, and roll back only with a note in the house rules.

**How to join**
1. In the launcher: Realms > Community > TODO(owner): <realm name> (the row says "Seeded · community-run").
2. Add your original-era game folder if you haven't: the realm card says which version it needs.
3. Press **PLAY**.

**Help carry it**
- Run a community node: https://leylinelauncher.com/wrath/docs/be-a-node
