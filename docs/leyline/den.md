# Your Den

Your Den is your own white world to build in, on your PC. It runs on a small local server the launcher starts for you, so it needs no realm and no internet once it is set up. Build a home, a hideout or a whole village, save it, and invite a friend in.

## Open it

1. Open the Leyline launcher and log in.
2. In the play bar's realm list, pick **Your Den** (it sits next to Play).
3. Press **Play**. The first time, the launcher downloads the small Den program, then signs you in and takes you into your Den.

## Build

A bar at the top of the screen has everything:

- **Build** (F7). Place anything from the catalog: search, then move, turn or delete it. `.den place chair` puts a chair in front of you. `.build undo` takes the last change back.
- **Shape ground.** Opens Forge, the world editor, on your Den's ground: raise hills, dig, paint. Your game shows the new ground after a restart.
- **Play** (F8). Hides the tools so you just walk your Den.
- **Save.** Your Den saves itself as you go. `.den save mine` keeps a copy under a name; the launcher's **Saved Dens** opens it again.

`.den grow` gives you more space. Type `.den` in your Den for every command.

## Invite a friend

**Invite** makes one link for one friend. They open it in their launcher and join you through the Leyline network. The launcher's **Invite friends** does the same.
