# Getting started

New here? [Start here](/wrath/start) has the four ways in, one click each: play in your browser, get the launcher, make your Den, host a server. This page is the longer guide.

Leyline plays with game files you already have. We never download, host or link to the game. This page gets you from nothing to playing on the official realm, Wrath Unleashed, in a few minutes.

## Four steps

1. **Get your game files.** Find the game folder on your PC, or put back a copy you kept. See [Where your files usually are](#where-your-files-usually-are).
2. **Add your folder.** In the Leyline launcher, press **PLAY** or pick **Add a game folder…** in the play bar. The launcher reads the version itself.
3. **Pick a realm.** The official realm is picked for you. The play bar's realm list also has **Your Den** (your own white world to build in, one click), the realms you host or played on, and **Host a server…**; **Realms** lists every realm.
4. **Play.** Press **PLAY**. The launcher points your game at the realm and keeps your original settings as a backup.

No files at all? You can still play in your browser: see [Browser or your own client](#browser-or-your-own-client).

## Where your files usually are

The right folder holds the game's program and a `Data` folder.

| On | Look in |
|---|---|
| Windows | `C:\Program Files (x86)`, `C:\Games`, your Desktop or Downloads |
| Mac | `Applications` or your home folder |
| Linux | `~/Games`, or a Wine, Lutris or Bottles prefix under `drive_c` |

Other places worth a look: an old drive or PC, a backup, or a cloud copy. Put the whole folder back on this PC, then choose it. A friend with the same version can also copy their folder to you, on a USB drive for example.

The launcher has the same tips on its **Find your game files** page: open it from the play bar's client list, or press **PLAY** with no folder added.

## Browser or your own client

- **In your browser.** Pick **Play in your browser** in the launcher's realm list, or open [leylinelauncher.com/wrath](https://leylinelauncher.com/wrath). Nothing to install, no game files needed. The browser streams Dalaran, the capitals, the starting areas, arenas, dungeons and raids. Drop your own game folder on the game page to go anywhere: see [Play](play.md#use-your-own-game-folder-to-go-anywhere).
- **Your own client.** The whole world, at your PC's full speed. Add your folder in the launcher and press **PLAY**. The official realm needs the Wrath-era game {{v:3.3.5a (12340)}}; other realms say which version they need.

Both use the same realm, the same account and the same characters.

## Your Den

Your Den is your own white world to build in, on your PC. Open the launcher, go to **Your Den** and press **Play solo**. One click: the launcher starts your Den on this PC, signs you in and takes you into the world. The first time, it downloads the small Den program first. No internet or sign-in is needed after that.

In your Den a bar at the top of the screen has everything:

- **Build** (F7). Place anything from the catalog: search, then move, turn or delete it. Undo takes the last change back. In chat, `.den place chair` puts a chair in front of you, and `.den stamp inn now` a whole inn. `.build undo` takes the last one back.
- **Shape ground.** Opens Forge, the world editor, on your Den's ground: raise hills, dig, paint. The first time, the launcher gets Forge for you. Your game shows the new ground after a restart.
- **Play** (F8). Hides the tools so you just walk your Den. F8 again brings them back.
- **Save.** Your Den saves itself as you go, and the bar says when. **Save** keeps a copy under a name, like `.den save mine`. The launcher's **Saved Dens** opens it again, or sends it to a friend.
- **Invite.** Makes one link for one friend. Send it: they open it in their launcher and join you here, through the Leyline network. The launcher's **Invite friends** does the same.
- **Help.** The short intro again.

`.den grow` gives you more space when you need it. Your game's own key bindings are kept in your Den. Type `.den` in your Den for every command.

## Level 1 or boost to 80

A new character on the official realm picks once:

- **Level 1**: start fresh and level up the usual way.
- **Boost to 80**: a one-time boost with a spec kit, professions and a trip to Dalaran, to play the end game straight away.

## Help and lost files

Ask on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/RfChVr8J7T). People there help find a game folder, restore one from a backup, and get you playing. In the game, type `.wu` for every command.

## Common fixes

- **The launcher says the folder isn't a game folder.** Choose the folder that holds the game's program and the `Data` folder, not a folder inside it.
- **The realm needs a different version.** The play bar says which one. Add that game folder, or pick a realm for the one you have.
- **The game can't connect.** Check the status dot in the launcher's title bar. If the realm is up, delete the `Cache` folder next to the game and start it again.
- **You changed the realm by hand before.** Realms › **…** › **Use my own realmlist** puts back what you had.
- **Wrong account or password.** Sign in at [leylinelauncher.com/wrath](https://leylinelauncher.com/wrath): the account page shows your game account.
- **Still stuck?** The [Discord](https://discord.gg/RfChVr8J7T), or the [Forum](https://leylinelauncher.com/wrath/leyline/forum).
