# Play with friends

**Status, 27 Nov 2026:** friends on the same network **work today**, and Your Den works from far away with two
router ports open. Inviting a friend from anywhere with no router settings is **coming**
([the way forward](way-forward.md)).

What to click today, step by step, and what will change.

## Before you start

Both of you need:

- **Leyline**, from the [download page](/wrath/download).
- **Your own game folder**, the same version, added under **Game folders**. Game files never travel between you.

No account and no website. Your launchers know each other by their own keys.

## 1. Add each other as friends

1. One of you opens **Single player**, then the **Friends** area, and presses **Show my friend code**. It is copied.
2. Send the code (`LF1-…`) by message or Discord. It works for a day.
3. The other pastes it under **Add a friend** and presses **Add**.
4. The first one presses **Accept**.

On the same network you can skip the code: press **Ask** on their name under **People near you**.

## 2. Start your world

- **Single player or the original world:** on the **Single player** page, press the big button. Wait until your
  game is in.
- **Your Den:** press **Play solo** on Home, or **Open my Den** on the Your Den page.

## 3. Invite your friend

**On the same network** (the same router):

1. On your Friends list, press **Invite** on your friend.
2. Your friend presses **Join** on your name within a day.
3. Their game logs in by itself. Their character stays on your world: next time they press **Join again**.

If you don't see each other, check that your firewall lets Leyline talk on private networks.

**From far away, Your Den only, today:**

1. In your router, send ports **3724** and **8085** (TCP) to your PC.
2. In Your Den, under **How your friend gets in**, type your internet address in **Your own address** and press
   **Save**.
3. Press **Invite a friend** and send the link to one friend.

The router steps are in [Invite a friend to your Den](den-invite.md#2-your-own-address).

**From far away, Single player or the original world:** not yet. That invite needed our server, which closed. Until direct joins land, play together on the same network, or in Your Den.

## 4. Keep it running

Your world is there while Leyline is open and your PC is awake. Set your PC's sleep to **Never** while friends play.
When you close Leyline, your friends' game stops too.

## When something goes wrong

- **The friend code doesn't work.** It lasts a day. Ask for a new one.
- **You don't see each other on the same network.** Same router? Firewall allowing Leyline on private networks?
  Use a friend code instead.
- **Your friend far away can't reach your Den.** Check both ports are sent to this PC, and that your line has its
  own internet address (some lines share one, and ports can't be opened on those).
- **Still stuck?** Ask on the [Leyline Discord](https://discord.gg/RfChVr8J7T).
