# The way forward

**Status, 27 Nov 2026:** a plan. The parts marked **Works today** are in the launcher now. Everything else is
**Coming**, in the order below.

Leyline's own server closed on 26 Nov 2026. This page says what Leyline becomes without it: a launcher that needs
no server of ours for anything. Not to log in, not to find friends, not to keep your characters.

## Why

When our one server went, so did sign-in, our own realm, the realm list that filled itself and the way friends
far away reached your world. One switch turned all of that off. The plan is to build Leyline so that no single
switch, ours or anyone's, can do that again.

The rules we hold to:

- **Your PC is the home.** Your key, your friends and your characters live on your PC and on your friends' PCs.
- **No centre.** Every part has more than one place it can come from.
- **We keep nothing.** No keys, no emails, no internet addresses, no passwords.
- **Your files stay yours.** Leyline never downloads, hosts, shares or ships game files.

## What is changing

1. **The Passport.** You log in with a key that lives on your PC, not with an account on our website. Friends can
   help you get it back if a PC dies. [Read more](passport.md).
2. **Joining with no relays of ours.** Your launcher finds your friend's PC by their key and connects straight to
   it when it can. When it can't, a friend you both know, or community relays, pass the sealed bytes along.
   [Read more](joining.md).
3. **Worlds in three clicks.** **Host**, a template, **Start**. On a PC that has played before, under a minute.
   An invite link opens the launcher on **Join**.
4. **Characters that survive.** Your character is saved on your PC, signed by you and the host, with sealed backups
   on friends' PCs. If a world closes, you take your character to another one. [Read more](characters.md).
5. **A network with no centre.** Realm lists, releases and blocks come as signed records from many places at once:
   other launchers, a public lookup network, copies of our site, community relays, your home network and your
   friends.

## The order we build it in

| Phase | What | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Measure the basics: time a fresh PC, test two PCs as friends, take screenshots | We have the numbers to beat |
| 1 | The Passport | You log in with no server at all |
| 2 | Connections | A friend far away joins with no relays of ours |
| 3 | Worlds in three clicks | Host, template, Start, in under a minute |
| 4 | Characters that survive | A character moves to another world after its host stops |
| 5 | A network with no centre | Our site and our relays are off for a week and nothing breaks |
| 6 | Open doors | Other server software and other launchers can join in |
| 7 | A last review of the whole thing | Every page and every claim checked again |

Each phase ships on its own. This page and the pages it links say **Works today** or **Coming** for each part, and
change when a phase lands.

## What you can do now

- Keep your launcher's key safe. In **Settings**, **Your Leyline network key**, press **Save my key…** and keep
  the file somewhere other than this PC. That key becomes your Passport.
- Add your friends with friend codes. Those friendships carry over.
- Play with friends on the same network, or open your Den to friends with your own address.
- Ask questions on the [Leyline Discord](https://discord.gg/RfChVr8J7T).
