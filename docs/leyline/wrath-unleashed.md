# Wrath Unleashed notes

Everything that is different on Wrath Unleashed, in one place. In the game, the Leyline button on the minimap opens the same guide.

## Welcome to Wrath Unleashed

- A Wrath realm from level 1 with the things players ask for most: faster levelling, solo-friendly dungeons, one community across both factions.
- Experience is 3x. Reputation and profession skill-ups are 2x. Gold, drops and honor stay normal.
- Every new character can start at level 1 or take a one-time boost to 80.
- Stuck or found a bug? .stuck takes you home, .bug tells us what happened.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `.wu` | every command on the realm |
| `.qol` | what is switched on here |
| `.rules` | the house rules |

## Your first steps

- A new character picks how to begin: play from level 1 (riding and your race's mount included), or boost to 80 with your spec's gear, two professions and a start in Dalaran.
- Closed the window? It comes back at your next login at level 1, and .boost opens it any time below level 10.
- Death Knights are open from your first character.
- More race and class pairs than the original game: every pair the game has today, such as a Human Hunter, a Tauren Paladin, a Dwarf Shaman or a Gnome Priest. Each starts in its race's own lands with its class's gear. In your own game, PLAY from the Leyline launcher to see them on the create screen.
- Spells are learned for you as you level, and weapon and defense skills stay at their best.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `.boost` | the start window again (below level 10, once per character) |
| `.learnall` | learn every spell for your level now |
| `.xp off` | stop gaining experience (.xp on to level again) |

## Dungeons, solo or with friends

- Every dungeon and raid scales to the group you bring, even just you.
- The Dungeon Finder opens at level 10 and queues you alone if you like.
- Loot from everything nearby with one click.
- At 80: keystone dungeons that get harder each level. Talk to the keystone master in Dalaran.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `.solo` | which dungeons to run at your level |
| `.guide` | where to go at 80: dungeons, gear, raids, PvP |
| `.hub keys` | to the keystone master |

## Both factions, one community

- Group, raid, chat and join guilds with players of either faction.
- One auction house for both factions.
- A guild needs no charter signatures, and mail with items arrives at once.
- World chat can be bridged to our Discord: .discord off hides those lines for you.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `.discord off` | hide the Discord lines (.discord on brings them back) |
| `/leyline` | the Leyline realms and your friends |

## Duels and PvP

- The duel corner in Dalaran: health and cooldowns come back after every duel.
- Level 80 duels count for a rating and weekly points, with leaderboards on the site.
- The 1v1 arena: rated or for fun, queued from anywhere.
- Battleground and arena queues are announced in chat, so a small realm still finds games.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `.hub duels` | to the duel corner |
| `.duel stats` | your rating (.duel top: the best) |
| `.q1v1 rated` | join the 1v1 arena (.q1v1 unrated for fun) |

## Looks, transmog and collections

- Transmog: the Warpweaver changes how your gear looks. Call one to you anywhere.
- Collections (Shift-P) shows your mounts and pets. Appearances calls the Warpweaver.
- Free faction, race and appearance changes.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `.transmog portable` | call the Warpweaver to you |
| `/collections` | mounts, pets and appearances (Shift-P) |
| `.ov` | free faction, race and appearance changes |

## Hardcore and challenges

## Minigolf, modes and your Den

- Minigolf and minigames to play with friends, right in the world.

- The Workshop shares what players build.

| Command | What it does |
