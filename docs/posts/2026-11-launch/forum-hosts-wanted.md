## One click

Get the **Host pack** from https://play.openvanilla.app/wrath/host or the launcher's **Host** page, pick a ruleset, press **Start**.

The Host pack hosts a realm for the game client it finds on your PC: original era, burning-crusade era, or the third era. Rulesets for a realm that stays up:

- **Vanilla+**: the original world only, up to level 60, 1.5x experience. Made to stay up.
- **True Vanilla**: a plain realm for the original client.
- **Progression**: normal speed from level 1, each character moving through the three eras at their own pace.
- **Classic levelling**: the full world from level 1 at 3x, with an optional boost.

Already run a server? **Link my server** puts it on the list without changing it.

## How players reach you

- **Your server, your files.** The realm runs on your PC. The game data it needs is read from your own game folder, on your PC. Nothing is downloaded from us and nothing leaves your PC.
- **On the list in about a minute.** A public realm shows on the realm list about a minute after it connects. A private one is reached only with your invite link.
- **No port forwarding.** Your realm calls out over one connection. Nothing to open on your router, and nobody sees your home address.
- **The relay only connects players.** It passes players to your realm and never sends game files. Hosts never see player addresses, and players never see yours.

## What you get

- **A listing** on the realm list, on the website, in the launcher and in the game. Order is by players online (bots count for little), uptime and a small boost while you're new. No paid placement, no votes.
- **Invite links** for friends. Make a new one any time; the old one stops working.
- **A console** in the launcher: status, announce, message of the day, kick and ban, an allow list, a health check, and restarts that always give players five minutes' warning.
- **House rules** shown on the list and in the game before anyone joins.
- **Optional: your own Claude** helps run it. Connect it once on the Agents page; every change waits for your OK.

## What you need

Honestly:

| | |
|---|---|
| Docker | Docker Desktop on Windows or macOS, Docker Engine on Linux. Free. |
| Your own game folder | For the era you want to host |
| Memory | 8 GB in the PC (the realm uses about 4 GB). 16 GB if you still want to use the PC. |
| Processor | Any 4-core processor from the last ten years |
| Disk | About 30 GB free, an SSD if you can |
| Network | A cable to your router is best. Wi-Fi works but drops more. |
| Time | The first start takes 5 to 20 minutes. Original-era and burning-crusade-era realms build on your PC the first time, which can take an hour or more, once. |
| Always on | Your PC stays on and awake. |

Renting instead? A small Linux server with 4 GB of memory, 2 cores and 30 GB of disk is enough. Copy your own game folder to it first.

The step-by-step for keeping a realm up (sleep settings, Docker at start-up, updates, power cuts): https://play.openvanilla.app/wrath/docs/always-on

## Get listed, get featured

1. Start your realm as **public**. It lists itself; there's nothing to apply for.
2. Claim it with your Hero Key so it stops showing as "Unclaimed" (the website gives you a one-time code; run `leyline claim CODE`).
3. Post it in **Share your realm**: the name, the ruleset, your house rules, and when you're usually on.
