## 2. Reddit post

**Title:** I made Leyline Solo: the first era on your own PC, with bot companions and co-op (free, open source)

> TODO(owner): one or two lines on why you made it, in your words.
>
> **Leyline Solo** is a free Windows app that runs a first-era world on your own PC, from your own game folder.
> No server to set up, no account to make.
>
> **What it does**
>
> - Install it, point it at your game folder, press Play. The first setup reads the maps from your folder once (up to
>   an hour); after that you're in within a minute.
> - It logs you in by itself and takes you to your characters.
> - Companions: `.bot group` gives you a party of four at your level. Order them in party chat (`follow`, `stay`,
>   `attack`, `flee`), or bring your own alts along with `.bot add Name`.
> - Characters in one list. Pick one and Play.
> - Invite a friend: one link per friend. Same network: straight in. Anywhere else: through the Leyline relay, nothing
>   to open on your router.
> - Offline once it's set up. Your world and your characters stay on your PC.
>
> **What it isn't (yet)**
>
> - You need your own first-era game folder. Leyline Solo never downloads, ships or uploads any of the game.
> - Windows only for now.
> - The server and the bots are open source projects built from source; some quests or dungeons may not behave yet.
>   Tell us and we'll fix what we can.
> - Your antivirus may ask about the server programs the first time: they're new to it. They're built in the open on
>   GitHub.
>
> **Links**
>
> - Download and source: <release page>
> - The page, with the guide and the questions: <site page>
> - Help and bug reports: <discord>
>
