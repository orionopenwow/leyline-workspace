# Build anything: the sandbox roadmap

Wrath on Leyline is heading toward a sandbox: a world where players build, script and share their own spaces and games, right in the browser.

## 1. Builder (now, MVP)

Players with the builder role place props from a catalog of chairs, tables, barrels, crates, lamps, banners, tents, fires and fences. Then they nudge each prop, turn it, and undo mistakes. Building happens in build areas, and the realm checks every action. A build can be saved and shared as a six-letter code that anyone else with the builder role can paste where they stand. Use `/builder` for the window, or `.build help` in chat.

## 2. Logic Editor (next)

Wire props together with simple triggers and actions: a door that opens when a lever is pulled, a lamp that lights at dusk, a sign that greets visitors. The editor will be visual, with no code to write.

## 3. Game-mode kits

Ready-made rules to drop into a build: a race track with checkpoints and a timer, capture the flag, hide and seek, a duel arena with a scoreboard. Kits combine Builder props with Logic Editor wiring, so anyone can change them.

## 4. Your own models (GLB importer)

Bring your own 3D props as `.glb` files, made by you or licensed for sharing. They are checked, credited and streamed to players, with no client patch to install.
