WrathUnleashed - how to connect (first test batch)

You need your own World of Warcraft 3.3.5a (build 12340) install. We don't provide game files.

Your login: go to https://play.openvanilla.app/wrath and sign in with Google or your Hero Key.
The page shows your account name and password. Sign in there again any time to see them.

Option 1 - your normal 3.3.5a client
  1. Open Data\enUS\realmlist.wtf (or Data\enGB\...) in Notepad.
  2. Replace the line with:   set realmlist play.openvanilla.app
  3. Delete the Cache folder next to Wow.exe, start Wow.exe, log in with the account from the page above.

Option 2 - in your browser (Chrome or Edge; needs WebGPU)
  1. Unzip this folder anywhere.
  2. Double-click "Play WrathUnleashed.bat". The first time it asks for your WoW 3.3.5a folder.
  3. Keep the black window open while you play. Your browser opens the game.
  4. Log in with the account from the page above.
  It only reads your own game files and only on your PC.

In game: .ov (free faction/race/look changes), .armory or /armory (gear), .qol, /qol.
