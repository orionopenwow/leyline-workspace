    FROM THE GAME

      Run a realm on this PC for friends or everyone. It uses your own game files. Players reach it through the Leyline network: you open no ports, and nobody sees your address. It runs while your PC is on.

      You need: Docker (a free program that runs your realm in its own space), your own game folder 3.3.5a, about 10 GB free. The first start takes 5 to 20 minutes, and your PC must stay on for others to play.

      1. Make your realm on the website. Sign in, pick a name, a ruleset and your house rules, then download your realm file.
      2. Get the Host pack (above), or unzip it yourself and show Leyline where it is. You also need Docker Desktop.

        Find your game files

        Leyline plays with a game folder you already have. Leyline can't download the game for you.

        DROP IT HERE

        Drop your game folder, or a zip of it, anywhere on this page. Or paste a direct link to your own backup zip.

        LOOK IN THE USUAL PLACES
        • Windows C:\Program Files (x86), C:\Games, your Desktop or Downloads
        • Mac Applications or your home folder
        • Linux ~/Games, or a Wine, Lutris or Bottles prefix under drive_c

        The right folder holds the game's program and a Data folder.

        RESTORE A COPY

        An old drive or PC, a backup, or a cloud copy: put the whole folder back on this PC, then choose it.

        ASK A FRIEND

        A friend with the same version can copy their game folder to you, on a USB drive for example.

        NO FILES?

        Play in your browser: the official realm streams there, with nothing to install.

        STILL STUCK?

        Ask on the Discord: people help find or restore files.
