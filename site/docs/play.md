# Play

With the server closed, there are three ways to play with Leyline, all with your own game folder:

- **[Single player](single-player.md):** your own world on your PC, with companions. No server, no account.
- **[Your Den](den.md):** your own white world to build in, on your PC.
- **A community server** from the [realm list](/wrath/leyline/realms): servers run by other people.

## On a community server

1. Make an account on the server's own website. The realm list links it. Leyline does not make accounts or keep
   passwords for any server.
2. In the launcher, open **Realms**, pick the server and press **PLAY**.
3. The launcher sets the realm list address in your own game folder and starts the game. Log in with the account
   you made on the server's site.

**Use my own realmlist** (Realms › **…**) puts back the address you had before.

## What closed

The official realm, playing in the browser (streamed or with your own folder), the guest pass, share links, the web
armory and sign-in on the website (the Hero Key and Google) all needed Leyline's own server. They closed with it in
November 2026.
