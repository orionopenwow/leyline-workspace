# Wrath Unleashed notes

Wrath Unleashed was Leyline's own realm. It closed with Leyline's own server in November 2026, and with it its
browser game, Duels, the arena queue and the leaderboards.

## Duels, Boss Rush and Your Den

- **Your Den** runs on your own PC and still works: see [Your Den](den.md).
- **Single player** runs on your own PC with companions: see [Single player](single-player.md).
- **Duels and Boss Rush on the official realm** and **the leaderboards** closed with it.

Other servers on the [realm list](/wrath/leyline/realms) run their own rules and features.
