Hey everyone! A big one, thanks to all of you who sent bug reports about playing on phones.

**Phones and tablets**
- Taps press buttons right away now, no more double tapping.
- The game goes full screen on your first tap.
- Add Leyline to your home screen and it opens like an app.

**Boss fights**
- Jump straight into a boss fight, no long dungeon run first.
- The Frozen Throne is the big one. Beat it and see how you rank.
- A clear-time leaderboard shows the fastest groups.

**Coming next**
- An Android app is in progress. More on that soon.

Play at https://leylinelauncher.com and tell us how it goes in #bug-reports or #ideas.
